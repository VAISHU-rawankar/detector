Interview Integrity Agent

This agent monitors your device only during the interview to help maintain integrity. It is not hidden — a tray icon stays visible the whole time, and it stops automatically when the interview ends.

While active, it reports the following metadata only:

  • Names of running applications and their window titles
  • Application code-signing publisher (to identify known AI tools)
  • Windows that hide themselves from screen capture
  • Virtual/loopback audio devices

It does not log your keystrokes, read your files, or capture your screen contents.
